## Becoming a maintainer

We welcome long-term contributors. Typical path:

1. Consistent high-quality PRs over several months
2. Review activity on others' PRs
3. Demonstrated ownership of an adapter, docs area, or subsystem
4. Invitation from existing maintainers (or self-nomination via an issue)

Maintainers are expected to:

- Uphold the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
- Review PRs in a timely manner (target: 5 business days for first response)
- Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerability handling
- Not merge their own PRs without at least one other review when a second
  maintainer is available
