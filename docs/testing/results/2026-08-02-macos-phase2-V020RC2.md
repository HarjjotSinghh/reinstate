## 12. Findings

### Release-blocking

### Non-blocking

1. **Live-corpus churn affects strict byte comparison of alias output.** Two
   `sessions --json` invocations seconds apart can differ in `updated_at` and
   `size_bytes` for unrelated sessions that are actively being written.
   Controlled records were identical across aliases and ordering was stable.
   Informational only.
2. **Ambiguous bare-ID resolution was not manufactured physically.** Producing a
